Maya privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Maya is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Maya has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. We never see your answers, your progress or your name.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, Maya stores the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • the name you choose during onboarding (used only in Maya's messages)
  • your answers, progress, streak ("Uptime") and spaced-repetition schedule
  • your settings, such as language, theme, sound and reminder time
  • card reports you write, until you share them

This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to share something (for example a Standup result or a card report) through your phone's share sheet. Then it goes only to the app and person you pick. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Maya's data in your device backup to your own Google account, so your progress survives a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your account; we never receive or see it. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Maya schedules local reminders on your device. No push service or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone's system settings.

Permissions

Maya asks only for notification permission, and only when you turn reminders on. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

Children

Maya is intended for students and professionals aged 13 and over and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example if an optional, privacy-respecting feature needs a server), we will update this page and the date above before shipping the change.
